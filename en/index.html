Texas Pathway

Study in the home town of Texas A&M

Bryan–College Station, Texas

The high school, Blinn College and Texas A&M are all about 20 minutes apart by car, so high school and university happen in one community.

Photos show the area for reference.

  1. STOP 1 · Grade 8–12

    High school in TexasCornerstone Christian Academy · Bryan, TX

    • Founded in 2002; an accredited private school from kindergarten to Grade 12
    • Small classes of about 12 students per teacher
    • Dual Credit with Blinn College in Grades 11–12; AP courses for strong students
    • National Honor Society (NHS) and end-of-year awards

  2. STOP 2 · 1–2years

    Blinn CollegeBlinn College District · Since 1883

    • About 20 minutes from Texas A&M; the best-known route to transfer there
    • 50+ programs and more than 18,000 students
    • Entry: TOEFL 61, high school transcript and diploma (published guidance)
    • Top students are invited to the National Society of Leadership and Success (NSLS)

  3. ARRIVAL

    Texas A&M · U.S. universitiesCollege Station, TX and beyond

    • Transfer from Blinn College to Texas A&M
    • Corps of Cadets scholarship up to 75%
    • Acceptances across the U.S., including Rutgers, Ohio State and SUNY
    • Example career path: Blinn nursing → U.S. nursing licence (NCLEX-RN)

Eulogia, Chiang Mai

It starts in Chiang Mai, one child at a time, at their own pace

A placement test on entry finds each child's level, and students then learn at their own pace in an American curriculum, moving on only after mastering each step. English and study habits are built here before the move to the U.S.

  • Opened August 2013 · American curricula SOT (ACE) and Abeka
  • ACE Quality Status school since 2015
  • U.S. high school diploma available through LCA dual enrollment
